Straight answers

The questions clients actually ask before sending a first message.

You have few reviews. Why should I take the chance?

Because the risk sits on my side, not yours. Work through escrow or milestones, judge the 48-hour draft before releasing the bulk of the payment, and check the demos above — they are real running code, and the source is public on GitHub.

How fast can this actually go live?

A landing page is typically 2–5 days end to end. A store is 4–10 days depending on catalog size. A bot with automations is 3–7 days. Rush delivery is possible and is quoted separately.

Do you use AI tools to build this?

Yes, and I am upfront about it. AI speeds up the boring parts; the architecture, testing and final quality are mine. You get readable code, a real handoff document and a person who answers when something breaks.

What if I need changes after launch?

Two revision rounds are included inside the agreed scope. After that, small changes are quoted per task, or you can keep me on a light monthly retainer.

Who owns the code and the accounts?

You do, fully, from final payment onward. Everything is created under your accounts wherever possible, and I hand over credentials at the end.

What do you need from me to start?

The goal of the project, one or two reference links you like, your deadline and your budget range. That is enough for a fixed quote. Content and assets can arrive while I build.
